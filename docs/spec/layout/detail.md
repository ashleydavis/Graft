# Layout

## Overview

Every page of Graft shows in the same layout, so a user always finds the same things in the same place. A top bar runs across the top of the window. Under it, from left to right, are the left sidebar, the main content area and the right sidebar.

## Behaviour

- The top bar runs across the top of the window. At its left is a menu button and next to it the name "Graft". At its right is the keyboard shortcuts button, then the theme menu's button and then the button for the right sidebar. Just before the keyboard shortcuts button is the text "Ctrl+/: Show hotkeys", which reminds the user of the key that shows the keyboard shortcuts overlay (see [shortcuts-overlay](../shortcuts-overlay/detail.md)). It is small, muted and in the code font, as the style guide shows a shortcut, and it shows at all times. The keyboard shortcuts button shows a keyboard icon and, like the sidebar buttons, is the style guide's square icon button. A click shows the keyboard shortcuts overlay. Between Graft's name and the reminder, set off by a divider, is the breadcrumb trail. It holds only these.
- The breadcrumb trail shows where the user is. Every page has one. While a repo is open it starts with that repo on every page but the welcome page, shown by its folder's name with the repo's full path in its tooltip, so the top bar reads "Graft", the repo's name, then the page. While no repo is open it starts with the page. The page's step is the page's title: one size larger (`--text-lg`) and bolder (`--weight-bold`) than the other steps, in the main text colour, like the name "Graft". On every page but the repo explorer the trail ends with it. On the repo explorer page the open panes follow it, and the last of them is in the main text colour at the normal size in the medium weight. The other steps are in the muted text colour at the normal size (`--text-md`). On a path with no page of its own the trail ends with the repo, in the medium weight. A chevron separates the steps.
- A step with sibling pages to switch to is a drop-down: a click opens a list of its siblings with a search box that narrows them by name and says "No page matches" when none does. The list marks the page being shown with the selected colour. Picking another page opens it. Picking the page being shown only closes the list. A step with no page to switch to is plain text.
- Graft has one repo open at a time, so the repo step is plain text. The welcome page is Graft's start page, the same whichever repo is open, and has no sibling page, so its trail is "Welcome" alone, plain text, whether or not a repo is open. The repo explorer page is the only page about the repo, so its trail is the repo, then "Repo explorer", both plain text. The "Repo explorer" step stands for the repo details pane, where the page opens. Each pane open to its right adds a plain text step after it, in the order the panes show, so the trail ends with the rightmost pane: "Commits" for the commit pane, the commit's first seven hash characters for the commit details pane with the full hash in the tooltip and the file's name for the file pane with its path in the tooltip. With the changes pane open in place of the commit pane, the steps are "Commit", then the open file's name with its path in the tooltip. The steps follow the panes as they open and close, by click or by key. For example, with every pane open the trail reads "test-repo", "Repo explorer", "Commits", "3c4f50f", "notes.txt". The Help and About pages are the pages about the app, so the trail of each is the repo, then a drop-down of Help and About.
- A step longer than 18 characters is cut to 17 and an ellipsis. Its tooltip holds its full text.
- The trail stays on one line inside the top bar at every window size. When its steps do not all fit, it keeps its first and last steps and puts one collapsed step, an ellipsis button, in place of the steps after the first. It collapses them one at a time from the step after the first, so the steps nearest the page shown stay longest, and only as many as it needs to fit. The collapsed step's tooltip gives how many steps it hides, such as "Show 3 hidden steps". A click opens a list under it of the hidden steps in the trail's order, each with its full text. Escape or a click outside the list closes it. When even every step between the first and last collapsed does not fit, the steps left are cut short with an ellipsis, and each one's tooltip holds its full text. Widening the window, or zooming out, brings collapsed steps back. The collapsed step is the style guide's small icon button and its list uses the drop-down's colours, in the light and dark themes.
- The left sidebar runs down the left of the window, under the top bar. At its top it holds a "Welcome" link to the welcome page, with a house icon, so the welcome page is one click away from every page. Under it is a "Repo explorer" link to the repo explorer page, shown only while a repo is open. At its bottom it holds a "Help" link to the Help page and an "About" link to the About page. Each link has an icon. The link to the page being shown is highlighted.
- The left sidebar is expanded the first time Graft starts: each link shows its icon and its name.
- The top bar's menu button minimises the left sidebar to a thin strip that shows each link's icon only, and expands it again. The links work in both states. Each link's name shows as its tooltip.
- The main content area fills the space between the sidebars and shows the current page: the welcome page, the About page, the repo explorer page or the Help page.
- The right sidebar is at the right of the window, under the top bar. It is closed the first time Graft starts. The top bar's button for it slides it in from the right edge and slides it back out. It shows the Git commands the open page has run (see [git-commands](git-commands/detail.md)).
- The theme menu picks the colours Graft is drawn in: Light, Dark or System, which follows the system's theme and changes with it. Its button shows the theme in use as an icon, a sun for Light, a moon for Dark and a half filled circle for System, with a small chevron that marks it as a drop-down. A click opens the menu under the button, aligned to its right edge. The menu is headed "Theme" and has a row for each setting, in the order Light, Dark, System. Each row shows the setting's icon, its name and a line saying what it does: "Light colours all the time", "Dark colours all the time" and "Follows your system's theme". The setting in use is marked with the selected colour. Choosing a row closes the menu and changes the theme. Clicking outside the menu or pressing Escape closes it and changes nothing. Graft starts on System. The choice is saved as `theme` in Graft's config file, so the next run starts on it, and a change to that setting while Graft runs changes the theme at once.
- Whether the left sidebar is minimised and whether the right sidebar is open are saved in Graft's state file, as `sidebar.left.minimised` and `sidebar.right.open`, each time the top bar's buttons change them. Graft starts with both sidebars as they were left. A missing or unreadable value leaves the left sidebar expanded and the right sidebar closed.
- Each button's tooltip shows what a click does next: "Minimise the left sidebar" or "Expand the left sidebar", "Open the right sidebar" or "Close the right sidebar", for the theme menu's button the theme in use, such as "Theme: System. Choose a theme", and for the keyboard shortcuts button "Show keyboard shortcuts" followed by its key, Ctrl+/, in the code font and fainter, as the style guide's tooltip with a shortcut shows it.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] The app has a top bar, a left sidebar, a right sidebar and a main content area, laid out with the top bar across the top and the left sidebar, main content area and right sidebar side by side under it.
- [x] The About and repo explorer pages show in the main content area.
- [x] The left sidebar starts expanded, and the top bar's menu button minimises it to a strip of icons and expands it again.
- [x] The left sidebar links to the repo explorer page, the Help page and the About page, in both states. The repo explorer page link shows only while a repo is open.
- [x] The right sidebar starts closed, and the top bar's button slides it in and out.
- [x] The top bar and sidebars hold no menu items or buttons for features Graft does not have.
- [x] The top bar always shows "Ctrl+/: Show hotkeys" between the breadcrumb trail and the keyboard shortcuts button.
- [x] The top bar has a keyboard shortcuts button, between the reminder and the theme menu's button, whose tooltip names Ctrl+/ and whose click shows the keyboard shortcuts overlay.
- [x] The top bar shows the breadcrumb trail of every page, starting with the open repo, when there is one, and ending with the page shown. The welcome page's trail is the exception: it is "Welcome" alone.
- [x] The About and Help pages' trails end with a drop-down of Help and About that opens the page picked. The repo explorer page's trail is plain text.
- [x] The page step of the trail is the page's title, a size larger and bolder than the other steps, and the trail still fits the top bar.
- [x] The top bar's theme menu starts on System and offers Light, Dark and System, each with its icon and what it does, marks the one in use and draws Graft in light or dark colours to match the choice, and on System follows the system's theme.
- [x] The theme setting is saved in Graft's config file, and Graft starts on the saved setting.
- [x] Whether the left sidebar is minimised and whether the right sidebar is open are saved in Graft's state file, and Graft starts with both as they were left.
- [x] The left sidebar has a Welcome link at its top that opens the welcome page from every page, the About and Help pages among them, whether the sidebar is expanded or minimised.
- [x] The welcome page's trail is a plain text "Welcome" step alone, with no repo step before it, whether or not a repo is open.
- [x] On the repo explorer page the trail has one step per open pane, in order, up to the rightmost, after the "Repo explorer" step.
- [x] The commit's step shows its first seven hash characters and a file's step shows the file's name, with the full hash or path in the tooltip.
- [x] The pane steps follow the panes as they open and close, the changes pane's "Commit" step among them.
- [x] The trail stays on one line inside the top bar at every window width. When its steps do not fit it keeps its first and last steps and collapses only as many steps after the first as it needs into one ellipsis step.
- [x] Clicking the collapsed step lists the hidden steps in order with their full text, and Escape or a click outside closes the list. Its tooltip gives how many steps it hides.
- [x] A step with less room than its text is cut short with an ellipsis and its tooltip holds its full text. Widening the window brings collapsed steps back.
- [x] The window and page are the style guide's background colour, `--color-bg` in `graft.css` (`#FAFAFA` in the light theme and `#09090B` in the dark theme), from the first paint.
